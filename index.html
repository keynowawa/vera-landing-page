The VERA Mission

E-commerce is broken. To prove you are a legitimate buyer, you have to surrender your privacy.

The Problem: Traditional Verification

Today, getting a "Verified Buyer" badge requires you to log into an account. This links your public review directly to your private email, real name, and purchase history. Your data is harvested, profiled, and often sold.

  • Requires an account login
  • Exposes your real identity
  • Links your reviews to your purchase history
  • Merchant stores all your data centrally

The Solution: Cryptographic Privacy

VERA Vault decouples verification from identity. Using advanced cryptography, VERA mathematically proves you own a valid receipt for a product, completely anonymously. You get the badge; they get zero personal data.

  • No account login required for reviews
  • Your real identity remains hidden
  • Reviews cannot be linked back to you
  • Data stays in your local browser Vault

1. You buy a product

During checkout, the merchant creates a Verifiable Credential. Think of this as a highly secure, digitally signed receipt. It contains the Product ID and a cryptographic signature proving the merchant issued it.

Merchant Signature: VALID

2. Secure Local Storage

The merchant sends this credential directly to your VERA Vault browser extension. It is stored locally on your hard drive. It is never uploaded to a central VERA server or cloud database.

Location: LocalStorage / Chrome Extension

3. Time to Review

Weeks later, you decide to leave a review. On the merchant's website, you type your review and click the "Verify Anonymously with VERA" button. The website asks your Vault: "Can you prove this user bought this item?"

4. Zero-Knowledge Proof (The Magic)

Your Vault looks at the receipt and generates a Zero-Knowledge Proof (ZKP). This is a complex mathematical puzzle. It proves the receipt is authentic and matches the product, but it mathematically strips away your identity and order number.

Generating ZK-Proof... Done.

5. Verified & Anonymous

Only the Proof and your review text are sent to the merchant. The merchant verifies the math. The math checks out. Your review is published with a "Verified Buyer" badge, but your identity remains completely unknown.

Technical FAQ & Privacy Commitments

Simple answers to complex cryptographic questions.

When people hear "Wallet", they think of cryptocurrency, Bitcoin, or Apple Pay. VERA does not store money. It is a secure, isolated container (a Vault) that stores identity credentials and digital receipts.

BBS+ is an advanced cryptographic signature scheme. Unlike standard signatures (like RSA), BBS+ allows for Selective Disclosure. This means if a merchant signs a receipt with 10 pieces of information (Name, Order ID, Price, Product ID), you can prove the signature is valid while choosing to only reveal the Product ID, keeping the other 9 pieces completely hidden.

We use Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKPs) based on Bilinear Pairings. Without getting too deep into the math, generating a proof is like mixing paint colors. It's easy to mix blue and yellow to get green, but if you hand someone a bucket of green paint, it is mathematically impossible for them to un-mix it back into the original colors. The proof verifies the math without exposing the original ingredients.

  • We do not operate central servers that track your activity.

  • We do not collect telemetry or analytics on your purchases.
  • The VERA codebase is entirely open-source and auditable.
