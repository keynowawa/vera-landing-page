Privacy Policy

Your Data Stays Yours

VERA Vault is designed from the ground up to prevent data collection. We believe privacy is a fundamental right.

What We Collect

Nothing. We do not collect analytics, telemetry, usage data, or any personal information. The extension does not make network requests to any VERA-operated server.

Local Storage Only

Your Verifiable Credentials are stored in Chrome's local storage API. This data exists only on your device and is never synchronized, uploaded, or shared.

Zero-Knowledge Proofs

When you verify a review, mathematical proofs are generated locally on your device and sent directly to the merchant's website. These proofs do not contain your name, email, IP address, or any information that could identify you.

What Merchants Receive

Merchants receive only: (1) the cryptographic proof that you purchased the product, and (2) your review text and rating. They cannot determine your identity from this information.

Data Deletion

You can delete any credential or reset the entire Vault at any time. Uninstalling the extension also removes all stored data.

Privacy-preserving review verification powered by Zero-Knowledge Proofs and BBS+ Signatures.
