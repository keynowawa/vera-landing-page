2. Description of Service

VERA Vault is a privacy-preserving browser extension that stores digitally signed purchase credentials locally on your device and generates Zero-Knowledge Proofs for anonymous review verification.

3. Privacy-First Architecture

VERA does not operate central servers for storing user data. All credentials are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage. We do not collect, transmit, or process any personal information.

4. Acceptable Use

The cryptographic proofs generated by VERA are intended solely for verifying authentic purchases. Any attempt to forge, manipulate, or misuse credentials is strictly prohibited.

5. Data Ownership

You retain full ownership of all credentials stored in your Vault. You may delete individual credentials or reset the entire Vault at any time through the Settings page.

6. Limitation of Liability

VERA is provided as-is for research and educational purposes. The developers are not liable for any loss of data resulting from browser updates, extension removal, or device changes.
